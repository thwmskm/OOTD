# 👕 YU*YL – Your Unique Daily Look

A mobile fashion and self-expression app designed to help users explore their personal style, document daily outfits (OOTDs), and curate their own digital wardrobe.

### 💫 The Meaning Behind YU*YL

YU*YL (유일) is a Korean word meaning **"one and only" or "unique."** It reflects the app's philosophy that personal style is individual, and there's no wrong way to express yourself through fashion.

The name also plays on **"YU" (You) + "일" (Day)**, highlighting the app's core experience: documenting your unique style, one day at a time.

## ✨ Features

- 📸 **Daily OOTD Uploads** – Capture and share your daily outfits to document your fashion journey.
- 📅 **Weekly Outfit Strip** – View your outfits from the current week in a convenient visual layout.
- 🗓️ **OOTD Calendar** – Browse past outfit posts through a calendar-based view.
- 👚 **Digital Closet** – Upload, organize, and manage your personal clothing collection.
- 🎨 **Outfit Curation** – Build and curate outfits using items from your digital wardrobe.
- 🔍 **Wardrobe Organization** – Sort and filter clothing items to easily navigate your collection.
- 🔥 **OOTD Streaks** – Track consecutive days of outfit uploads and maintain consistency.
- 📊 **Style Statistics** – View personal style statistics and track your fashion activity.
- 🌤️ **Daily Weather** – View daily weather information alongside your outfit planning.

## 🏗️ Architecture

The application follows a cloud-connected mobile architecture:

1. **Frontend:** React Native and Expo provide the cross-platform mobile experience.
2. **Navigation:** Expo Router handles application routing and screen navigation.
3. **Authentication:** Firebase Authentication manages user registration and login.
4. **Database:** Cloud Firestore stores user data, wardrobe items, and daily OOTD records.
5. **Image Storage:** Firebase Storage handles clothing and outfit image uploads.
6. **State Management:** Zustand manages shared application state across components.

## 💡 Project Highlights

- Developed a cross-platform mobile application centered around personal fashion and self-expression.
- Integrated Firebase services for authentication, cloud database management, and image storage.

- Built a date-based OOTD journaling experience with weekly and calendar views.
- Developed user engagement features through outfit streaks and personalized style statistics.
- Utilized Zustand for centralized application state management.
